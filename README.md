# The Profit: 3 Best Episodes, Ranked

This website showcases my top 3 episodes of the CNBC show "The Profit" using basic HTML. No CSS, no JavaScript, just pure HTML for a minimalist experience.

**Featured Episodes:**

1. **Sweet Pete's (Season 2, Episode 6)**
   - Summary: This episode highlights Marcus Lemonis's ability to navigate complex family dynamics while revitalizing a struggling candy business. The tension between the owners and their partner, combined with Marcus's direct approach, creates a compelling narrative.

2. **Shuler's BBQ (Season 2, Episode 15)**
   - Summary: In this episode, Marcus assists a family-owned barbecue restaurant in expanding its operations while preserving its traditional values. The challenges of growth and maintaining quality are central themes.

3. **Amazing Grapes (Season 3, Episode 24)**
   - Summary: This episode features a couple who have transformed their personal wine collection into a failing business. Marcus guides them in transforming their passion into a profitable venture.
